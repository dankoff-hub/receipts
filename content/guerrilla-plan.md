# Receipts — Free Guerrilla Marketing Plan (TikTok)

## Goal
Get 10,000+ views on the Day 1 video ("They said I wasn't ready") in 7 days, $0 spend.

## Tactics (ordered by effort/impact)

### 1. Comment hijacking on big accounts (15 min/day)
- Find 5–10 accounts in career/motivation/fitness with 50k–500k followers.
- Comment on their newest videos within 30 min of posting.
- Comments must be value-add, not "check my page". Example:
  - "This is why I keep a private receipt of every time someone doubted me. 30 days of proof hits different."
  - "Spite is a terrible finish but a great start. I turned a rejection into a 30-day standard and it changed everything."
- Goal: their audience clicks your profile.

### 2. Duet/Stitch bait
- Post a video that ends with a question: "What's the one thing someone said that you still keep as a receipt?"
- Encourage replies. TikTok boosts videos with high comment rates.

### 3. Trend-jacking
- Use TikTok's Discover page daily. If a trending sound or format fits (even loosely), remake your video with it.
- Speed matters: trend lifespan is 24–72 hours.

### 4. Cross-post to Instagram Reels
- Same video, same caption. Buffer Free supports 3 channels.
- Instagram Reels has different audience overlap and can drive TikTok profile visits.

### 5. Reddit seeding (value-first)
- Post in r/GetMotivated, r/selfimprovement, r/careerguidance with a genuine story, not a link drop.
- Example: "I got passed over for a promotion. Instead of complaining, I wrote a 'receipt' and turned it into a 30-day standard. Here's what happened." Then mention the system in comments if asked.

### 6. Niche hashtag stacking
- Mix 1 trending tag + 2 niche tags + 1 branded tag per post.
- Example: #provethemwrong (niche) + #careertok (niche) + #fyp (trending) + #receipts (branded)

### 7. Reply-to-comment video
- When someone comments on your video, reply with a video response.
- TikTok prioritizes comment replies in the algorithm.

### 8. QR code guerrilla (physical)
- Print 50 small QR code stickers linking to receipts-blond.vercel.app/bio.
- Place them in high-traffic areas: coffee shops, co-working spaces, gyms, university campuses.
- Keep them small and mysterious: "They said I wasn't ready. Scan to prove them wrong."

### 9. Micro-influencer barter
- Find 10 creators with 10k–100k followers in your niche.
- Offer them the $29 pack for free in exchange for an honest review or duet.
- No cash, just product access.

### 10. TikTok LIVE (if eligible)
- Go live for 15–30 minutes discussing the "receipt" concept.
- LIVE videos get pushed to followers and can attract new viewers.

## Metrics to track
- Views per post
- Profile visits from TikTok
- Link clicks to /bio
- Comments per post
- Follower growth rate

## Weekly review
- Which comment got the most profile visits?
- Which hashtag drove the most views?
- Which cross-post performed best?
- Double down on what works, drop what doesn't.
